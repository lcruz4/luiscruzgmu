How it works

From public records to money in your hands

Here is what to expect from your first call to the day your funds are released.

1

We review public records

We look through county, court and foreclosure or tax sale records to find sales that left money unclaimed.

2

We confirm who is owed

We verify the former owner or heirs and reach out with a free review of what we found.

3

We help you file the claim

If you choose to proceed, we prepare and file everything and keep you updated until you're paid.

1. Free review

When you request a review, we look up the sale and check whether surplus funds are being held and who may be entitled to them. We reply within 1 business day, even if no funds exist.

2. Written agreement

If there is a claim and you want our help, we send a written service agreement that explains our fee and what we will do. Nothing starts until you sign it. You can cancel at no cost before a claim is filed.

3. Documents and filing

We tell you exactly which documents your case needs, such as ID, your deed, or estate paperwork for heirs. We prepare the claim and file it with the right county office or court.

4. Follow-up until payment

We track the claim, answer questions from the county or court, and keep you updated. Timing depends on the county or court. Many claims take a few months from filing to payment.

5. You get paid

Once the funds are released, our fee is paid from the recovered amount as written in your agreement. You owe nothing if no funds are recovered.
