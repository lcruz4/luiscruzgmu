About us

Helping families get back money that is already theirs

Golden Recovery Group is a private surplus funds recovery company. We find money left over from foreclosure and tax sales and help the people it belongs to claim it.

Who we are

[Short story of who started Golden Recovery Group and why. Mark to provide.]

Every year, counties and courts hold money from foreclosure and tax sales that sold for more than was owed. Many former owners and heirs never learn the money exists, and unclaimed funds can eventually be lost to the state. We find those cases in public records and help the rightful owners through the claim.

What we believe

  • Money that belongs to someone should go back to them. That's the whole reason we exist.
  • You should understand every step. We explain what we found and what happens next in plain language before you sign anything.
  • You shouldn't pay to find out. Reviews are free, and our fee is paid only if your funds are released.
  • Your information is private. We use it only to work on your claim and never sell it.

Where we work

We serve former owners and heirs in all states except California, New York and Arizona.
