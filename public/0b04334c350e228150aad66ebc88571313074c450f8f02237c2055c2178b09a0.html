Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are surplus funds?

If a home sells at a foreclosure or tax sale for more than the debt and costs owed, the difference is held by the county or court. That money may belong to the former owner or their heirs.

How did you find my information?

Foreclosure and tax sale results are public records. We review them to identify people who may be owed money.

Are you a government agency?

No. Golden Recovery Group is a private company. We are not affiliated with any court, county or government office.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. We are paid only if your funds are released, and the fee is agreed in writing first.

Which states do you serve?

All states except California, New York and Arizona.

How soon will I hear back?

We reply to every new request within 1 business day.

How long does it take?

It depends on the county or court. Many claims take a few months from filing to payment.

What documents will I need?

Usually a photo ID and proof of ownership or heirship, such as your deed. We'll tell you exactly what your case needs.
