Eligibility

Could you be owed surplus funds?

You may have a claim if a property you owned, or one owned by a family member who has passed away, was sold at a foreclosure or tax sale.

Who may be eligible

  • Former owners whose home or land was sold at a mortgage, HOA or tax foreclosure sale.
  • Heirs of a former owner who has passed away, including through an estate or probate.
  • Co-owners who were on the title at the time of the sale.

When surplus funds exist

Surplus funds exist only when the sale price was higher than the debt, interest, fees and costs owed. If the property sold for less than what was owed, there is usually no surplus.

States we serve

We work with claimants in all states except California, New York and Arizona.

Deadlines matter

Each state and county sets its own rules for how long surplus funds are held. After the deadline, the money may go to the state or county instead of you. If you think you may have a claim, it's best to ask soon.

What helps us review your case

  • The property address and county
  • A copy of your deed, if you have it
  • A copy of your promissory note, if you have it
  • For heirs: a death certificate and any estate or probate paperwork

Don't have these? That's fine. We can often find what we need in public records.

You can file on your own.

In many places you can claim surplus funds directly from the court or agency holding them without using a recovery company. Our services are optional.
